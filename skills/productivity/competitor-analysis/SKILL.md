# Competitor Analysis

Analyze competitor SEO and GEO strategies to find gaps and opportunities.

## Process

1. **Identify competitors**: direct, indirect, and content competitors.
2. **Gather data**: domain, traffic estimates, authority, business model, audience, offerings.
3. **Analyze keywords**: rankings, top keywords, intent mix, gaps.
4. **Audit content**: volume, top performers, themes, publishing patterns.
5. **Review backlinks**: totals, quality, top domains, linkable assets.
6. **Assess technical**: Core Web Vitals, mobile, architecture, internal linking.
7. **Check GEO**: which queries cite competitors, what formats get cited, openings.
8. **Synthesize**: comparison table, strengths to learn, weaknesses to exploit, keyword opportunities, content recommendations, and action plan.

## Output

- Executive summary
- Competitor comparison table
- Keyword and content opportunities
- Backlink gaps
- Technical strengths/weaknesses
- GEO citations analysis
- Immediate / short-term / long-term plan

## Tips

- Analyze 3-5 competitors.
- Include indirect players.
- Study both strengths and failures.
